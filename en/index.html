FAQ

Frequently asked questions

06
Can I train with the coach one-to-one? +

Yes. The app offers a one-to-one session over video — half an hour or an hour, from $29: technique review, program adjustments, your questions. The courses themselves need no coach involvement: the program adapts on its own.

How do I get access to a course? +

Leave your email on the course page and pay. Access opens automatically right after the payment — the course shows up in the app under that email. A bought course is yours for life. The club with a task every day comes together with the course by subscription, monthly or annual.

What is the email code? Do I need a password? +

There is no password. To sign in you enter your email, receive a six-digit code by email and confirm it. If the email did not arrive, check spam; you can request a new code after a minute.

Which devices does the app work on? +

Any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install: open the link and sign in. Progress is stored in your account, so you can switch devices freely.

Do I need equipment? +

No. Forma. Start uses no equipment: a mat and a sturdy chair are enough. If a movement is not there yet, the app swaps it for a simpler one.

I am a beginner. Where do I start? +

With Forma. Start — it is made for beginners and for anyone coming back after a break. On first login you answer five short questions, and after your second workout the app offers a test of five movements: squats, push-ups, sit-ups, lunges and a plank. It uses them to fine-tune your level and load.

Can I get a refund? +

Yes, within 14 days of activation if you have completed fewer than 3 workouts of the course. The refund policy explains how.

Where do I write if something does not work? +

Email hello@forma-app.co. Mention the email you ordered with so we can find the order quickly. We answer in the order received.
