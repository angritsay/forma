Frequently asked questions

How is my share counted? +

On Start you keep 80% of course and club sales and 90% of one-to-one sessions. On Pro, 90% and 95%, plus a fee of $59 a month. Counted on the amount after the Prodamus or lava.top fee.

How does Forma get its share? +

On Pro the money lands in your account, and how and when we settle is set in the contract before launch. On Start payments go through Forma and your share is paid out monthly.

Who sets the prices? +

We agree on them together and set them in the contract. Prices on this page are Forma’s current ones, as examples.

Who owns the content, and is it exclusive? +

Content ownership and exclusivity are agreed and set in the contract.

What about taxes and paperwork? +

The legal form and taxes are agreed and set in the contract.

I can’t edit video +

You don’t need to. You film — we cut, label and grade the clips in Studio.

What languages is the app in? +

Russian and English — each person picks their own.

Will my people see Forma? +

On Pro, no: the domain, the bot, the app and the payment receipt are all under your name. On Start your page and course live inside the Forma app.

What about the App Store and Google Play? +

Possible, for an extra fee — discussed separately. Without the stores the app opens from a link and installs on phone and computer.

Everything I have is on GetCourse — what about it? +

Leave it. Launch something new on Forma — a club, say — and look at the numbers. Moving over is discussed separately.
