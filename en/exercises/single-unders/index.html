Single-unders

Single-unders are one rope turn per jump. They are the most efficient home cardio there is: minute for minute the rope burns more than a moderate run while training the calves, feet, coordination and rhythm. We use them in warm-ups and as the breathing element in metcons. Jump low, land softly and turn the rope with your wrists, not your whole arms.

How to do it

  1. 1

    Check the length: standing on the middle of the rope, the handles should reach your armpits. Hold the handles with elbows tucked to your sides, rope resting behind your heels.

  2. 2

    Turn the rope with small circles of the wrists and hop over it with both feet, 3–5 cm off the floor.

  3. 3

    Land on the balls of your feet with soft knees, torso upright, eyes forward.

  4. 4

    Keep an even rhythm: one turn, one jump, with no extra bounce between turns.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Turn with the wrists
  • ✓ Jump low
  • ✓ Elbows in
  • ✓ Soft on the balls of the feet

Common mistakes

  • ✕ Jumping too high and tucking the legs
  • ✕ Turning the rope from the shoulders
  • ✕ Landing flat-footed or on the heels

Breathing

Steady breathing in time with the jumps; never hold your breath.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Single-unders? +

The most common ones: jumping too high and tucking the legs; turning the rope from the shoulders; landing flat-footed or on the heels. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Single-unders? +

Keep these cues in mind: turn with the wrists; jump low; elbows in; soft on the balls of the feet.

How should I breathe during Single-unders? +

Steady breathing in time with the jumps; never hold your breath.
