Jog in place

Jogging in place is the simplest way to raise your body temperature and heart rate before a workout. It gently prepares the leg joints, calves and feet for jumps and squats and suits every level. It is the base option for anyone not yet ready for high knees or jumping jacks.

  • Beginner
  • No equipment

How to do it

  1. 1

    Stand tall, feet hip-width apart, elbows bent.

  2. 2

    Start a light jog in place, lifting one foot at a time and landing on the ball of the foot.

  3. 3

    Swing your arms as in running, trunk upright and shoulders relaxed.

  4. 4

    Gradually pick up the pace over the prescribed time so your breathing is noticeably faster by the end.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Light on the feet
  • ✓ Relaxed shoulders
  • ✓ Build the pace

Common mistakes

  • ✕ Stomping flat-footed
  • ✕ Leaning forward

Breathing

Calm nasal breathing, switching to the mouth as you speed up.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Jog in place? +

The most common ones: stomping flat-footed; leaning forward. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Jog in place? +

Keep these cues in mind: light on the feet; relaxed shoulders; build the pace.

How should I breathe during Jog in place? +

Calm nasal breathing, switching to the mouth as you speed up.
