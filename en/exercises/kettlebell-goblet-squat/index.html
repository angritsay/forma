Kettlebell goblet squat

The kettlebell goblet squat is a squat with the bell held by the horns at your chest. A kettlebell is more comfortable than a dumbbell here: the centre of mass sits closer to your body and the elbows naturally drop down to push the knees out at the bottom. It is the main squat of the kettlebell course, building quads and glutes and teaching a deep, upright squat with a load in front.

How to do it

  1. 1

    Hold the kettlebell by the horns (the sides of the handle) against your chest, elbows pointing down. Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out.

  2. 2

    Brace, push your hips back and sit down, knees tracking over your toes.

  3. 3

    Descend to parallel or lower; elbows pass between the knees, chest up, heels down.

  4. 4

    Drive through the whole foot and stand, fully extending the legs and squeezing the glutes.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Bell to the chest
  • ✓ Elbows push the knees out
  • ✓ Chest up

Common mistakes

  • ✕ Bell drifting away from the chest and the back rounding
  • ✕ Knees caving inward
  • ✕ Heels lifting off the floor

Breathing

Inhale before the squat, exhale as you stand.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Kettlebell goblet squat? +

The most common ones: bell drifting away from the chest and the back rounding; knees caving inward; heels lifting off the floor. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Kettlebell goblet squat? +

Keep these cues in mind: bell to the chest; elbows push the knees out; chest up.

How should I breathe during Kettlebell goblet squat? +

Inhale before the squat, exhale as you stand.
