Farmer carry

The farmer carry is walking with heavy dumbbells or kettlebells in both hands. The most real-life exercise in the program: it trains your grip, traps, core and posture exactly the way grocery bags and suitcases do. The technique is simple and it is almost impossible to get hurt. At home, walk back and forth across the room for the prescribed time — keep moving until the timer ends.

How to do it

  1. 1

    Place the weights beside your feet, squat down with a flat back to grip them and stand up as in a deadlift.

  2. 2

    Stand tall: shoulders down and back, chest open, abs braced, arms straight at your sides.

  3. 3

    Walk with steady, short steps without swinging the weights or leaning to either side.

  4. 4

    When the time is up, stop, squat down with a flat back and set the weights on the floor.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Shoulders back and down
  • ✓ Crush the handles
  • ✓ Walk tall along a line

Common mistakes

  • ✕ Shoulders shrugged toward the ears
  • ✕ Leaning sideways or slouching
  • ✕ Dropping the weights at the end

Breathing

Even breathing in time with your steps; do not hold your breath.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Farmer carry? +

The most common ones: shoulders shrugged toward the ears; leaning sideways or slouching; dropping the weights at the end. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Farmer carry? +

Keep these cues in mind: shoulders back and down; crush the handles; walk tall along a line.

How should I breathe during Farmer carry? +

Even breathing in time with your steps; do not hold your breath.
