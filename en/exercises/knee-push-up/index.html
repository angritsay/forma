Knee push-up

Knee push-ups remove the weight of your legs from the movement, cutting the load on the chest and triceps by roughly a third compared with the full version. The technique is identical: straight line from knees to head, full range, elbows tucked. It is the official option in our push-up test and the first step toward push-ups from the toes.

How to do it

  1. 1

    Kneel and place your hands under your shoulders, feet back or ankles crossed. Body in a straight line from knees to head.

  2. 2

    Bend your elbows at about 45 degrees to your torso and lower your chest toward the floor without arching your lower back.

  3. 3

    Touch your chest to the floor or stop a few centimetres above it.

  4. 4

    Press back up to fully straight arms, keeping your hips down.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Hips forward, don't sit back
  • ✓ Chest to the floor
  • ✓ Elbows back, not out

Common mistakes

  • ✕ Hips drift back toward the heels so it becomes a bow, not a press
  • ✕ Lower back sagging
  • ✕ Short range of motion

Breathing

Inhale on the way down, exhale as you press up.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Knee push-up? +

The most common ones: hips drift back toward the heels so it becomes a bow, not a press; lower back sagging; short range of motion. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Knee push-up? +

Keep these cues in mind: hips forward, don't sit back; chest to the floor; elbows back, not out.

How should I breathe during Knee push-up? +

Inhale on the way down, exhale as you press up.
