Hip flexor stretch

The kneeling hip flexor stretch opens the front of the hip and the iliopsoas — muscles that shorten from sitting and from running, mountain climbers and leg raises. Free hip flexors mean a neutral lower back in the squat and full hip extension in hinges and jumps. Do it in the cool-down on each side; the time in the workout is per side.

How to do it

  1. 1

    Kneel on your right knee with your left foot in front so the left knee is over the heel. Put a mat or a folded towel under the knee if you like.

  2. 2

    Squeeze your right glute and tuck the pelvis (tailbone down) — you should feel the stretch at the front of the right hip.

  3. 3

    Gently shift your hips forward without arching the lower back; keep the trunk upright and, if you like, reach the right arm overhead.

  4. 4

    Hold for the prescribed time, breathing calmly, then switch sides.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Tuck the pelvis
  • ✓ Squeeze the back glute
  • ✓ Stay upright

Common mistakes

  • ✕ Arching the lower back instead of stretching the hip
  • ✕ Front knee pushed far past the toes

Breathing

Slow inhale, long exhale — sink a little deeper with each exhale.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Hip flexor stretch? +

The most common ones: arching the lower back instead of stretching the hip; front knee pushed far past the toes. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Hip flexor stretch? +

Keep these cues in mind: tuck the pelvis; squeeze the back glute; stay upright.

How should I breathe during Hip flexor stretch? +

Slow inhale, long exhale — sink a little deeper with each exhale.
