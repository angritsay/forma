Kettlebell deadlift

The kettlebell deadlift lifts the bell from the floor between your feet by extending the hips and knees with a flat back. It is the first kettlebell exercise we teach: it sets the hip hinge, the start position and the grip you will need in swings, cleans and snatches. With the bell between your feet the load sits ideally close to the body, so the technique comes easily even to a beginner.

How to do it

  1. 1

    Place the bell between your feet, feet slightly wider than your hips, toes slightly out.

  2. 2

    Push your hips back, bend your knees and hinge with a flat back; grip the handle with both hands, shoulders over the bell, chest open.

  3. 3

    Brace and stand up, extending knees and hips together — the bell travels straight up.

  4. 4

    Stand fully tall and squeeze the glutes at the top, then return the bell to the floor along the same path.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Flat back, chest open
  • ✓ Push the floor away
  • ✓ Bell close to the legs

Common mistakes

  • ✕ Rounding the back at the start
  • ✕ Hips rising before the shoulders
  • ✕ Leaning back at the top

Breathing

Inhale and brace before the lift, exhale at the top.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Kettlebell deadlift? +

The most common ones: rounding the back at the start; hips rising before the shoulders; leaning back at the top. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Kettlebell deadlift? +

Keep these cues in mind: flat back, chest open; push the floor away; bell close to the legs.

How should I breathe during Kettlebell deadlift? +

Inhale and brace before the lift, exhale at the top.
