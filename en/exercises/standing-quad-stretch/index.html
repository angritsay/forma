Mobility

Standing quad stretch

Quick facts

  • Beginner
  • No equipment
  • Quads
  • Hip flexors
  • seconds
See the course

The standing quad stretch is the cool-down after squats, lunges, step-ups and burpees. The heel is pulled toward the glute, knees together, hips pushed slightly forward — the quadriceps and the front of the hip stretch. Hold a wall or chair for balance; the time in the workout is per leg.

How to do it

  1. 01

    Stand side-on to a wall or chair and rest one hand on it.

  2. 02

    Bend the other leg, take hold of the foot and pull the heel toward the glute.

  3. 03

    Knees together, push the hips slightly forward — you will feel the front of the thigh.

  4. 04

    Hold calmly, breathe; then switch legs.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Knees together
  • ✓ Do not arch the lower back

Common mistakes

  • × Knee drifts out and the lower back arches

Breathing

A long exhale in the position.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Standing quad stretch? +

The most common ones: knee drifts out and the lower back arches. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Standing quad stretch? +

Keep these cues in mind: knees together; do not arch the lower back.

How should I breathe during Standing quad stretch? +

A long exhale in the position.
