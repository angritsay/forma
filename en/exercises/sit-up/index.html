Core: flexion

Sit-up

The sit-up is the classic dynamic abs exercise: from lying on your back you rise to a seated position and return. Unlike a short crunch it works the rectus abdominis and the hip flexors through a full range, which is why sit-ups are a staple of CrossFit workouts. Do them smoothly and without jerking so the spine stays safe and the abs get all the work.

How to do it

  1. 1

    Lie on your back with knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart, arms crossed on your chest or reaching forward.

  2. 2

    Tuck your chin slightly, brace your abs and start peeling your head, shoulders and shoulder blades off the floor, curling up one vertebra at a time.

  3. 3

    Rise all the way to sitting, chest toward your knees, feet staying on the floor.

  4. 4

    Lower back down along the same path until your shoulder blades touch the floor, then begin the next rep.

Coaching cues

  • ✓ Curl up, don't rise like a board
  • ✓ Chin tucked
  • ✓ Feet stay down

Common mistakes

  • ✕ Yanking with the arms and neck instead of using the abs
  • ✕ Dropping back down without control
  • ✕ Feet lifting off and the legs kicking up

Breathing

Exhale as you rise, inhale on the way down.

FAQ

What are the most common mistakes in Sit-up? +

The most common ones: yanking with the arms and neck instead of using the abs; dropping back down without control; feet lifting off and the legs kicking up. Watch for them from the first rep.

What should I focus on during Sit-up? +

Keep these cues in mind: curl up, don't rise like a board; chin tucked; feet stay down.

How should I breathe during Sit-up? +

Exhale as you rise, inhale on the way down.
