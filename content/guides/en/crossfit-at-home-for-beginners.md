CrossFit at home for beginners is not box jumps and a barbell overhead. It is three short sessions a week built on four movements you can learn in a living room: the squat, the hip hinge, the push-up and the plank. Every gym program starts from those same patterns, and you can own them safely in a two-by-two-metre space with a chair and a wall. If you are wondering how to start CrossFit at home, this guide covers what your first workout looks like, the mistakes almost every beginner makes and where to go once the basics feel easy.

## What you will get from this guide

- A plain explanation of what CrossFit for beginners actually is at home and how it differs from "doing some exercise".
- The six movements to learn before you add anything harder.
- A complete first workout with exact reps and rest — the same session that opens the Start course.
- The common mistakes and a progression plan for your first four weeks.

## CrossFit at home for beginners: where to start

CrossFit is general physical preparedness: strength, endurance, mobility and coordination trained in one program instead of four separate ones. In a gym that means barbells, rings and rowers. At home it means the floor, a chair and a wall. The principle does not change: basic movements, varied formats, gradual progression. Before you build a home workout plan for beginners, answer three questions honestly.

### How many days a week

Three sessions a week with a rest day between them is the right dose for a beginner. The ACSM recommends that novices leave 48–72 hours between sessions loading the same muscle groups — that is when the adaptation actually happens. The WHO guideline for adults is 150–300 minutes of moderate activity per week plus muscle-strengthening work on at least two days. Three 30-minute home sessions and a walk on the other days cover that without heroics.

### What equipment you need

None. A mat is optional. A sturdy chair gives you [incline push-ups](exercise:incline_push_up) and [step-ups](exercise:step_up); a wall gives you the [wall sit](exercise:wall_sit). Everything else happens on the floor. A bodyweight CrossFit session is not a compromise: until you can do twenty clean squats in a row a barbell would only get in the way, and twenty clean squats is your first goal.

### When to see a doctor first

Talk to a doctor before you start if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, joint or back pain, are pregnant, or have not trained in years and are over forty. A program should work around your limits, not pretend they do not exist.

## Six movements to learn first

A beginner CrossFit workout is built from six patterns. Learn each in its simplest version and every workout after that becomes accessible.

| Pattern    | Exercise                                 | What it trains                                |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Squat      | [Air squat](exercise:air_squat)          | Thighs, glutes, ankle mobility                |
| Hinge      | [Glute bridge](exercise:glute_bridge)    | Glutes, hamstrings, lower back                |
| Push       | [Knee push-up](exercise:knee_push_up)    | Chest, shoulders, triceps                     |
| Lunge      | [Reverse lunge](exercise:reverse_lunge)  | One leg at a time, balance                    |
| Core       | [Plank](exercise:plank)                  | Trunk stability, lower-back protection        |
| Locomotion | [Jog in place](exercise:jog_in_place)    | Heart rate, warm-up, transitions              |

The rule for the first two weeks: **no jumping**. Burpees, jump squats and rope work come once your knees and ankles have adapted to the volume. That is exactly how [the Start course](course:start) is built: no jumps in weeks one and two, and every later jump carries a step-based option in its note.

## Your first workout

This is day one of the Start course. With the warm-up and cool-down it takes about 30 minutes. The strength block is slow: two seconds down, one second up; if your form breaks, end the set early.

**Warm-up — 2 rounds, no rest:**

1. [Jog in place](exercise:jog_in_place) — 45 seconds, marching is fine.
2. [Arm circles](exercise:arm_circles) — 30 seconds.
3. [Squat to stand](exercise:squat_to_stand) — 6 reps.
4. [Inchworm](exercise:inchworm) — 4 reps.

**Strength — 3 sets, 60 seconds of rest between sets:**

- [Air squats](exercise:air_squat) — 10 reps, heels down, knees pushed out.
- [Incline push-ups](exercise:incline_push_up) — 8 reps, hands on the chair seat, body in one straight line.
- [Wall sit](exercise:wall_sit) — 20 seconds, thighs parallel to the floor or slightly higher.

**Core — 2 rounds, 30 seconds of rest between rounds:**

- [Dead bug](exercise:dead_bug) — 8 reps per side.
- [Plank](exercise:plank) — 20 seconds.
- [Glute bridge](exercise:glute_bridge) — 12 reps with a pause at the top.

**Cool-down:** [cat-cow](exercise:cat_cow) — 6 reps, [hamstring stretch](exercise:hamstring_stretch) — 40 seconds per leg, [child's pose](exercise:child_pose) — 45 seconds.

### How the app scales it

Before the course starts, Forma runs a short test: max knee push-ups in two minutes, max squats in one minute and a plank hold. Those numbers produce a fitness index, and the index sets your starting volume multiplier — anywhere from 0.6 to 1.3. A beginner with a weak test gets 6 squats per set instead of 10; someone arriving with a base gets 13. Before each session you can pick easier or harder (volume changes by 15 %, rest gets longer or shorter), and afterwards you rate the effort from 1 to 10. Finished everything at an easy effort? The next session grows a little. Struggled, or something hurt? The volume drops, and the app tells you to back off and to see a professional if the pain persists.

## Common beginner mistakes

1. **Starting with burpees and jumps.** Knees and ankles are not ready and the squat is not clean yet. Squat first, jump later.
2. **Skipping the warm-up.** Five minutes of [jogging in place](exercise:jog_in_place) and arm circles are not a formality; they are shoulder and lower-back injury prevention. How to build one is covered in [the warm-up guide](guide:warm-up-home).
3. **Chasing numbers.** Ten squats to parallel beat twenty half squats. Only clean reps count — in the test and in the session.
4. **Training every day.** Strength is built on rest days. Instead of a fourth session, walk: 7 000 steps counts as an active day in the app and keeps your streak alive.
5. **Quitting in week two.** The first result is not visible muscle; it is sets that stop feeling like punishment. Give the program four honest weeks, then retest.

## How to progress

Progress for a home beginner is not "more rounds"; it is a steady climb through harder versions of the same movements. Use this order.

- **Weeks 1–2.** Technique and volume: the same exercises with a few more reps. Incline push-ups become [knee push-ups](exercise:knee_push_up), the plank grows from 20 to 40 seconds, the bridge from 12 to 15 reps.
- **Weeks 3–4.** [Reverse lunges](exercise:reverse_lunge), [supermans](exercise:superman) and the first short AMRAPs of 6–8 minutes appear. What those formats mean and how to count rounds is explained in [the AMRAP, EMOM and Tabata guide](guide:workout-formats).
- **After four weeks.** Retest. If you squat noticeably more in a minute and hold the plank longer, the base is there. Next come [half burpees](exercise:half_burpee), [jump squats](exercise:jump_squat) and [the Bodyweight Engine course](course:engine) with four sessions a week.

If push-ups are your sticking point, there is a separate [push-up progression guide](guide:push-up-progression). And for building workouts from whatever is in the room, read [the guide to CrossFit without equipment](guide:no-equipment-crossfit).

## Wrap-up

CrossFit at home for beginners means three sessions a week, six basic movements and honest reps with no jumping for the first two weeks. Start with the test and the first workout of [the Start course](course:start): the app sets the volume for you and adjusts it from your feedback after every session.
